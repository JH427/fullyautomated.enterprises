## Historical note

This entry describes the earlier Rick/Greg workflow. Those agent identities are retired. It is preserved as historical context, not an Active experiment or a description of the studio’s current team.

## What the record supports

The May 2026 workflow asked what should survive a handoff between an analyst and an operator: the question, claimed findings, source trail, uncertainty, and requested action.

Its useful lesson was modest: a summary is not evidence. The next operator needs enough provenance to check a claim rather than inherit its confidence. The original record did not establish a reliability rate or a broad performance advantage.
